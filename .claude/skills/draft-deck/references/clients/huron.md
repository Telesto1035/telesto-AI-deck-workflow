# Client file: Huron

Read only when the paper is Huron's (the attached deck uses its template, theme or fonts). Everything here is specific to this client; the firm-wide rules are in `house-register.md` and `build-rules.md`. Where this file conflicts with a freshly attached shipped Huron deck, the attachment wins.

**Scope.** This file holds what the client publishes about itself or would recognize as house style: layouts, palette, fonts, table and chart formatting, voice register, claims precedent. It never holds engagement content: survey figures, quotes, non-public financial information, competitive reads, fee levels, strategic rulings about the client's business. Those stay in the engagement's ledger and never enter a skill file.

## Paper: layouts, fonts, palette
- **Layouts:** Cover Option 1 (cover); Callout Option 1 (the one-sentence takeaway page, image background, about 30 words at 36pt); Content Header Only, white (every page with an exhibit); 1_Content Header Only, aubergine side panel (low-text pages only: exec summary, quote pages, next steps; never a chart page); Divider Option 1 (appendix divider). Layouts with a numeric prefix carry a side panel; use them to vary rhythm, not by default.
- **Fonts:** Aptos Display 24pt titles on every content page, held to two lines (about 105 characters); Tahoma for body, tables and chart labels.
- **Palette:** aubergine 5D013A (theme accent 2) for header fills, highlighted bars, bold lead-ins and number chips; tint F5E6EF for the client's own row; band fill EBE9E4 for takeaway and context boxes; footnotes dark gray 5A5A5A or 7F7F7F (the accepted exception to the gray-text rule); teal for Telesto owners on next-steps pages; everything else ink.
- **Title placement:** about 0.4 inch from the top edge; number chips top-align with the first title line.

## Tables, charts, callouts
- **Tables:** one font size within a grid (11pt comfortable for 8 to 10 rows); header row aubergine fill with white bold text; percentage columns centered, name column left; the client's row in the tint with bold aubergine text; bold the maximum per column; cells top-aligned.
- **Charts:** native bars in one color (aubergine); percentages centered inside bars in white Tahoma 10pt regular; category labels as a column of left-aligned 10pt text boxes (native axis labels hidden), plot area pinned; value axis, gridlines, ticks, legend and chart title removed; a one-line subtitle above carrying metric and denominator ("% of respondents selecting, n=200, top 6 of 10 shown"). Gray chart subtitles are accepted. Competitor rows carry logos; the client's row sits in a dashed outline box.
- **Callouts:** takeaway, discussion and context lines on the band fill, no outline, text inset about 0.12 inch, edges on the column grid; register labelled ("Recommendation:", "What this means for Huron:", "For discussion:"). Bold the key phrase and the number in aubergine.
- **Stat callouts:** 26pt figure with the sentence at 10pt beside it.
- **Next steps page:** numbered aubergine squares, bold lead phrase per step, owners bold in aubergine (client) or teal (Telesto), generous row spacing, no subtitle above.
- **Exec summary:** at most 5 rows by 3 columns (findings, implications, recommendations); each point double-clicks into one or two body pages sharing the eyebrow number.

## Voice and claims precedent
- Findings written as Huron's opportunity, ending on Huron's position ("which is where Huron can highlight its track record").
- Bands may point to a named Huron product where one exists; the draft names the service and leaves the product call to the consultant.
- Cross-survey evidence is welcome when labelled: quotes from a sister survey under a head that names it, with question and n in the footnote.
- Section summary titles read as the section's question ("What the findings mean for Huron's AI report and marketing campaign"); no "Part 1 / Part 2" label on the slide.
- Claims precedent: match the assertion level of the shipped EDR and Public Sector decks. An escalation beyond that level is flagged, not made — and so is a draft that sits below it. On implication and next-steps pages, name the agent, the direction and who carries the consequence. A sentence that states a change without saying who causes it, which way it moves, or what it costs the client reads as too neutral here and comes back.

## Observed, not yet promoted
Single-deck observations waiting for a second deck or a ruling before entering the house register.
- Segment sentences end in what the segment will want to see, in plain words, with less data than the draft carried (EDR AI minipack, 2026-09-13).
- When two survey findings in a section say the same thing, one is replaced with a finding from another source in the pack that speaks to the same audience (EDR AI minipack, 2026-09-13).
- A page that argues one number shows it large: a 32pt stat and a "Buyers say:" quote replaced a three-column grid (Public Sector, 2026-09-12).
- How-to matrices use parallel imperative cells, "N/A" for empty cells, column heads as questions the reader asks ("How to reach", "How to compete") (Public Sector, 2026-09-12).
- Word count on shipped-ready pages ran 120 to 260 words, footnotes apart; one page above 300 drew "wordy" (EDR AI minipack, 2026-09-12). Now the gate's warn and block lines.
