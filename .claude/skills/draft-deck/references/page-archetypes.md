# Page archetypes

Observed across shipped work (EDR Institution Priorities v4, EDR Competitive Intelligence v4, Overall Research Recommendations v4.1 — client paper; Arkridge Homecare DD — Telesto paper). Map each dash of the dot-dash to one archetype. When more than one fits, ask: "Should this page be A) …, B) …, C) …, or D) something else?" with a recommendation.

Layouts below describe content structure. Colors, fonts, and furniture always come from the attached deck, never from this file.

## Deck skeleton (both papers)

Title page → Table of contents → Objectives & methodology → Executive summary → Detailed findings (sectioned, TOC repeated at each section break) → Appendix pointer → Thank you.

- TOC repeats before each section with the current section highlighted.
- Appendix pointer slide: one sentence stating what full backup exists and where ("Full survey crosstabs (n=250) are provided as a separate document, covering all 33 questions segmented by …").
- DRAFT stamp appears on every working content page; comes off only at final.

## 1. Executive summary — numbered finding + implication
The signature page. 4–6 numbered items. Each item:
- **Finding sentence** (bold lead): what the data shows, with the key numbers inline ("82% of respondents who worked with Huron rate the firm as top quality…").
- 1–3 supporting sentences with quantified detail.
- **Implication:** line — what to do about it. Separate line, labeled.

The item numbers carry through the deck: each detail slide shows its exec-summary number as a kicker, so every body page traces to one exec summary point. Preserve this linkage when adding or reordering pages.

## 2. Findings → Recommendations two-column
Recommendations variant of the exec summary: numbered rows; left column = finding (with data), right column = recommendation. Column headers "FINDINGS" / "RECOMMENDATIONS". Used as the front page of a recommendations deck.

## 3. Chart + takeaway rail
Main chart (~2/3 width) plus a side rail of 2–4 short takeaways ("Key takeaways" / "Key segment findings"), each 1–2 sentences and quantified ("VP/Directors prioritize financial sustainability far more than the C-suite (26pts difference)"). Chart carries a subtitle line with metric and denominator ("% of respondents selecting attribute · n=250") and a question-number footnote.

## 4. Dual-chart comparison
Two related questions side by side, each with its own denominator line and question footnote. A segment callout strip (1–2 quantified observations) sits below or beside. Used to juxtapose e.g. revenue levers vs. cost levers, academic vs. research trends.

## 5. Free-response / quotes page
Title states the base ("Findings from 89 free responses"). Left: category frequency chart of coded responses. Right: example verbatim quotes, each with attribution "(Board of trustees, R1)". Grouped under small topical headers ("On governance challenges"). Never edit quote wording; select and attribute.

## 6. Segment split
Two populations compared across 3–4 dimensions in a grid of paired mini-charts (e.g., "Boards vs. Execs" across awareness channels, thought-leadership demand, consideration factors). Legend states each group's n. Callouts quantify the gap.

## 7. Self-rating stack (Q&A page)
Rows: question text left, stacked-bar response distribution right, labeled with collapsed scale points ("Extremely/Very clear · Moderately · Slightly/Not clear"). Used for confidence/readiness batteries. Appendix-adjacent; light on interpretation.

## 8. Profile series
Identical layout repeated once per subject ("Huron | Perception, Strengths, and Value Proposition", then BCG, Bain, …). Title pattern: `Subject | fixed descriptor`. When building a series, build one, confirm it, then replicate.

## 9. Coverage / whitespace matrix
Rows = topics (with icons if the template has them), columns = sources or segments, cells = intensity + parenthetical examples ("Very high (endowment, deficits, closures)"). Bottom row calls out whitespace. Footnote states the corpus and window ("~180 recent articles … last 12 months").

## 10. Objectives & methodology
Two panels: methodology facts (who, n, how sampled, when) and target outcomes (icon + one line each). States margin of error and flags which cuts are directional ("At 95% confidence, margin of error is ±6.2 pts; functional area cuts (n=19 to 56) are directional only").

## Telesto-paper variants (from Arkridge DD)
- Exec summary as bullets with bold teal lead-ins on the key phrase, sub-bullets for supporting detail.
- Numbered-circle action list ("Identified quick wins post-activation"): 3–5 items, bold lead phrase + plain continuation.
- Scope two-column: "Analysis we carried out" vs. "Additional work required", numbered items with bold lead-ins.
- Logo/entity table: logo left, one factual descriptor right with the differentiating fact bolded ("~16 offices in TN").
- Source-category matrix: category row headers with icons, source logos, methodology bullets.
- Furniture: Telesto logo bottom-left, "©Telesto Strategy LLC 2026" + page number bottom-right, confidentiality line on title page only. No footers beyond this; minimal grey microtext.
