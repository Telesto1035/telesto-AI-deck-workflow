# House register: writing rules learned from shipped decks

Firm-wide rules for how Telesto writes a page, distilled from the edits consultants and the CEO made to AI drafts. Each rule is one line, one before-and-after pair, and its evidence. Client-specific wording and formatting live in `clients/<client>.md`, not here.

## Governance
- **Cap: 25 active rules.** A new rule above the cap retires one.
- **Entry:** a pattern enters after it recurs in two shipped decks, or on a CEO ruling. A single-deck observation waits in the client file under "Observed, not yet promoted".
- **Conflict:** the newer CEO ruling wins. The rule it replaces moves to Retired with the date and what replaced it. Two observed rules that conflict are both held until a ruling.
- **Refresh:** reviewed when Learn runs and at least twice a year; a rule no consultant edit has touched in a year is a candidate for retirement or for the principles file.
- **Format:** rule in bold, one example, then `[evidence: N decks | CEO ruling | owner ruling, YYYY-MM-DD]`. No narrative.

## Active rules (17)

### Titles
1. **A title states the finding, not the method, the label or the survey structure.** "Secondary analysis of competitor articles reveals whitespace for [topic]" became "On competitor websites, [topic] is scarcely covered by any of the six firms". [evidence: 2 decks, 2026-09]
2. **Interpretation leaves the title and goes to the band.** A title that ends ": the opening is [X], not the case for [Y]" loses the clause; the band carries the thought. [evidence: 2 decks, 2026-09]
3. **No label-colon opener, no abstract-noun subject, no compressed verb-noun, no prescription in a title.** "Adjacency quadrant: 35 markets" became "We ranked 35 service markets by size and closeness to the client's core"; "Entrenchment is buyer-specific" became "[Firm A], [Firm B] and [Firm C] hold established positions". [evidence: 1 deck, 5 titles; owner ruling, 2026-09-12]
4. **Fewer names in a title: the two that matter, and where they matter.** Four firms became "[Firm A] and [Firm B] hold the largest positions at [Buyer 1] and [Buyer 2]". [evidence: 1 deck, 2026-09-12]

### Claims and evidence
5. **Size the claim to the number; a ratio survives only when exact; a named firm as subject takes a softer verb.** "twice the prime-bid path" became "larger than the prime-bid path"; "has the least of it deployed" became "does not see it deployed actively". [evidence: 2 decks, 2026-09]
6. **Findings are written as the client's opportunity and end on the client's position.** "Leaders rank [topic] second" became "Leaders need help with [topic]"; bodies end on where the client stands. [evidence: 1 deck, 3 passes; consultant ruling, 2026-09-12]
7. **A negative finding carries its opportunity on the same page, under a neutral head.** Never "the good news" or "the bad news": "Where the client already stands" and the number. [evidence: consultant and owner rulings, 2026-09-12]
8. **Burning-platform framing at most once per deck, sized to the evidence.** Offer two strengths, the partner's and the data's; the consultant chooses. [evidence: consultant ruling, 2026-09-12]
9. **Method caveats leave client-facing pages.** Sub-share conventions, uncoded-interview notes, scope notes go to methodology or the appendix. [evidence: 1 deck, 5 pages, 2026-09-12]

### Recommendations
10. **One implication band per page, never one under each stat.** Seven "For the client:" lines became one band headed "What this means for the client:". [evidence: CEO ruling, 2026-09-13]
11. **A recommendation names the client's priority and carries the imperative in "should" or "needs", not a command verb, with no jargon nouns.** "Run [the three buyers] as named accounts" became "[The three buyers] are priority accounts that should be pursued with digital and program credentials". [evidence: 2 decks; CEO-approved proposals, 2026-09]
12. **Bands bold the two or three phrases that carry the action and drop meta lines about the deck itself.** "The wider report is where that association gets built" was cut. [evidence: 1 deck, 2 passes, 2026-09-12]
13. **Next steps are proposals the room decides on ("Discuss and select"), with owners and dates, and leave the naming of themes to the room.** [evidence: 1 deck, 2 passes, 2026-09-13]

### Numbers, sources, hygiene
14. **Figures from 10 upward, words below; fiscal-year ranges as "FY24-25".** "Seventeen of the thirty-five markets" became "17 out of 35 markets". [evidence: 1 deck, 7 instances, 2026-09-12]
15. **One source line per deck, stating what the source is, with no internal counts and no repository or dashboard names.** "310 coded quotes" and "Dashboard v27" were removed. [evidence: 1 deck, every page, 2026-09-12]
16. **Symbols and quotes read without a key.** A legend symbol is explained in words; a quote gets curly marks, a bracketed referent for a bare pronoun, and an attribution with the organization spelled out. [evidence: 1 deck, 2026-09-12]
17. **Words the skill owner removes on sight:** earned, already, carry / carries, load-bearing, recast, hypothesis, "this confirms [client]". [evidence: owner ruling, 2026-09-12; "carry" confirmed by a consultant edit]
