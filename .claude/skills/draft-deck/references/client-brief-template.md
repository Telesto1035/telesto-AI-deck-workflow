# Client brief template

One file per core client, kept in the client's shared engagement folder, never in this repo. The skills read it as optional context when it is attached or sits in the engagement folder. It is context, not evidence: no figure in it reaches a slide unless the deck's own sources carry it.

Two refresh triggers, both by a consultant: quarterly, after the earnings release (results, guidance, acquisitions, leadership, sections 1 to 4); and after any client meeting, section 6.

Copy from the line below. Two pages at most.

## 1. The company in one paragraph
What it sells, to whom, how it reports revenue, current-year guidance, headcount, stated medium-term goals.

## 2. Strengths and weaknesses, from the public record
Four of each, one line apiece, each traceable to a source in section 7. The first weakness is the one Telesto's work addresses.

## 3. Key departments and services
Segments or business units with the services each sells and named leaders where public; cross-cutting capabilities; corporate officers; where marketing sits.

## 4. Acquisitions and divestitures, last 12 months
Date, target, segment, what it adds.

## 5. Key account stakeholders
The teams Telesto interfaces with, at a high level: team, where it sits, three or four responsibilities from job postings or public pages. Roles, not names, unless the name is public in that role.

## 6. Stated objectives (Telesto's record; update after meetings)
Goals and challenges in the client's words: competitor to beat, perception to shift, line to grow, market to enter, what they hired Telesto to find out. Then a dated log:

| Date | Meeting | Who (role) | What they said | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | open / done / superseded |
